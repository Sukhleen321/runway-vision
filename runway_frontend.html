From raw frame to three located lines, in one forward pass.

A MobileNetV3 backbone feeds two heads — one for the runway mask, one for the six anchor points.

01

Frame in

A single RGB image is resized to 256×256 and normalized — no manual cropping or annotation needed.

02

Dual-head inference

The segmentation head marks the runway surface; the regression head predicts six anchor points — left edge, right edge, and centerline endpoints.

03

Lines out

Predicted points are scaled back to the original image resolution and drawn as green, red, and blue overlays.

Validated on a held-out 733-image set.
